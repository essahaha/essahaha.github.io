• Windscribe Email Copywriting

    Brief:

    Take on Windscribe’s distinctive tone of voice and write a welcome email for someone who has just subscribed to its mailing list. This is not necessarily someone who has purchased a Windscribe plan. They have simply entered their email address and agreed to hear more from the brand.
    Your email should:
    ● Welcome the reader to Windscribe’s mailing list.
    ● Introduce the brand’s personality.
    ● Give them a taste of the strange, funny emails they can expect.
    ● Make them feel pleased—or slightly concerned—that they subscribed.
    ● Include a clear call to action encouraging them to explore Windscribe or download the VPN.

    Tone of Voice:

    Your copy should feel: ● Funny
    ● Tongue-in-cheek
    ● Irreverent
    ● Self-aware
    ● Slightly unhinged
    ● Direct and conversational
    ● Unexpectedly honest
    ● Anti-corporate
    Windscribe can tease the reader, make jokes about surveillance or acknowledge that this is a marketing email.
    However, do not rely entirely on random humour. The email should still clearly communicate what Windscribe does and give the reader a reason to engage with the brand.
